# Project Review: Swasth Saans (स्वस्थ सांस)
## TB Clinical Workflow System — AI-Assisted Decision Support & Screening

> **"Jaldi Pehchaan, Behtar Samadhaan" (Early Identification, Better Solution)**

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**Swasth Saans** is a futuristic, high-performance clinical decision support system designed to streamline the screening and management of Tuberculosis (TB). Built with a focus on early identification, it provides healthcare workers with an AI-driven interface to assess patient risks, analyze geographic hotspots, and manage clinical workflows in multiple languages.

---

## 2. Core Vision & Mission
Tuberculosis remains a critical public health challenge, often delayed by complex clinical workflows and lack of centralized risk assessment. Swasth Saans aims to:
- **Accelerate Identification**: Use AI to flag high-risk patients during the first point of contact.
- **Support Frontline Workers**: Provide an intuitive, multilingual tool for clinicians and healthcare volunteers.
- **Data-Driven Public Health**: Visualize TB clusters to enable targeted interventions.

---

## 3. Key Features & Modules

### 🧪 Intelligent Screening Wizard
A streamlined 5-step process that guides health workers through the clinical workflow:
1.  **Demographics**: Captures essential patient data, including occupational and contact history.
2.  **Chief Complaints**: Dynamic logging of symptoms (fever, weight loss, hemoptysis) and visit frequency to detect "delayed diagnosis" patterns.
3.  **Investigations**: Simulation of OCR-powered prescription reading and X-ray analysis to extract historical clinical data.
4.  **AI Risk Report**: Generates a categorized risk assessment (Low/Moderate/High) with actionable clinical recommendations.
5.  **Test & Booking**: Integrated referral system for CBNAAT/Sputum microscopy and follow-up appointments.

### 📍 Hotspot & Analytics Dashboard
- **Regional Insights**: Identifies geographical clusters (e.g., Dharavi/Kurla region) where high-risk cases are peaking.
- **Trend Analysis**: Visualizes daily screening volumes and urgency metrics.
- **Urgency Alerts**: Real-time listing of cases requiring immediate clinical intervention.

### 💬 AI Clinical Assistant (Chatbot)
- An integrated multilingual chatbot that provides instant answers to clinical protocols, FAQ, and platform navigation, ensuring health workers are never stuck.

### 🌎 Multilingual Support
- Fully localized in **English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Marathi (मराठी)**, ensuring accessibility across diverse linguistic demographics.

---

## 4. Design & User Experience (UI/UX)
Swasth Saans is not just functional; it is designed to feel like a premium, state-of-the-art medical tool:
- **Glassmorphism Aesthetic**: Transparent layers and frosted glass effects for a modern, clean interface.
- **Dark Mode Optimization**: Neon accents (Cyan for primary actions, Red for urgent alerts) provide high contrast and reduced eye strain for clinicians.
- **Interactive Backgrounds**: Particle-based animated backgrounds powered by Three.js create a dynamic, "living" application environment.
- **Mobile-Responsive**: Optimized shell that adapts seamlessly to desktop or tablet use in clinics.

## 6. Clinical Value Proposition
*   **Reduced Diagnostic Delay**: Automated alerts for repeated visits and chronic cough help prevent patients from "falling through the cracks."
*   **Standardized Care**: Ensures every health worker follows the same thorough screening protocol.
*   **Actionable Data**: Moves beyond simple data entry by providing clear "Immediate Actions" (e.g., Provide mask, urgent referral).
*   **Resource Management**: Helps authorities deploy resources (like mobile screening vans) to high-risk areas identified via Hotspot Analysis.

---

## 7. Conclusion
**Swasth Saans** is a bridge between advanced AI analytics and frontline healthcare. By Combining a stunning, intuitive UI with a rigorous clinical workflow, it transforms how TB is screened and managed, ensuring that every breath is a healthy one.
